{# The Category / Storage Location / Sub-Location inputs. Included by product/_form_fields.html (the add and edit forms) and by product/capture.html, so the two pages offer one vocabulary rather than two that agree until someone edits one of them (018 FR-007, FR-008). Each page keeps its own card wrapper; only the fields live here. Reads `values`, a mapping of field name to current value. capture.html names its context `form_data` and binds it with {% with values = form_data %}. The element ids are a contract, not decoration: field-autocomplete.js binds #location and #sub_location by id on DOM ready, and catalog-suggestions.js fills #category-suggestions by id. Renaming one silently switches its suggestions off. #}
Slash-separated, any depth. Typing a new one creates it -- there is no setup step.
