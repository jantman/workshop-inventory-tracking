Quantity On Hand is your own count. Leave it blank and this product is not tracked at all; enter 0 to record that you have none. Receiving a purchase never changes the count. Reorder Threshold is the count at or below which the product page flags it as low; leave it blank for no threshold, and note that 0 is a real threshold meaning "flag it once I have none". A threshold only signals for a product whose quantity you are actually tracking. Stock Status is only ever what you said — nothing in the system sets it for you. Low and Out of stock make the product read as low on its own page whatever the count and threshold say, or even with neither filled in; OK does not cancel a threshold you have already crossed. The date shown beside it is when you last changed it, not when you last saved the product, so editing anything else here leaves it alone; setting the status back to Not set clears that date. Location and Sub-Location share their suggestions with the inventory items.
